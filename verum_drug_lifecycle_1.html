Product strategist note: The standard 4-phase clinical trial framing misses where the real commercial evidence decisions happen. This map adds 2 pre-trial phases (Target Selection → Evidence Strategy) and 3 post-approval phases (HTA Submission → Pricing & Reimbursement → Post-market / Label Extension). These 5 phases are where HEOR teams spend disproportionate time and where current tooling is weakest. Verum's leverage sits almost entirely in this expanded perimeter — not inside the clinical trial itself.

Manual consulting (€50–200K, weeks). EVERSANA NAVLIN (partial, proprietary model). No public-corpus mining tool exists.
Well served · 72%
Medidata, Veeva Vault, CTMS platforms. Strong tooling. HEOR barely involved at this stage.

eClinical platforms. Covidence/DistillerSR for SLR. No HTA-aligned endpoint planning tool for this stage.
Well served · 72%
Medidata, Veeva, DistillerSR, Covidence, EPPI-Reviewer. Most crowded segment in pharma tech.
Well served · 75%
Veeva Vault Regulatory. PAREXEL, Syneos CRO support. Strong tooling exists. Entirely separate from HTA workflow.
Critical gap · 12%
Word/Excel manual workflows dominant. DistillerSR (SLR only). No evidence→dossier infrastructure. Dominant method: HEOR consultant hours billed per country.
Low · 20%
Specialist pricing consultancies (IQVIA, Evidera). Health economic tools (TreeAge, R). No integrated platform connecting evidence to pricing inputs.
Partial · 45%
Flatiron, IQVIA RWD, Datavant. Strong RWE platforms but fully disconnected from HTA dossier workflow.
Very low · 8%
Full restart of manual HEOR workflow for every new indication. No cross-indication evidence reuse infrastructure exists anywhere in the market.
Verum
Out of scope
Long-term Phase C opportunity: evidence graph could surface which indication/population combos have the strongest historical HTA acceptance rates.
V2 — Core value prop 🎯
Mine published HTA decisions → extract implied PICO per country → output minimum covering evidence set and country gap analysis. The evidence strategy question nobody has answered as infrastructure. Replaces €50–200K consulting engagements.
Out of scope
Clinical operations space. HEOR barely present. No Verum angle.
V2 — Signal
End of Phase II: HEOR asks "which endpoint will HTA agencies accept?" Verum's PICO database answers from historical decisions — informing Phase III design before it's locked and costly to change.
V1 — Baseline
EvidenceUnit structuring of the SLR component. As trials complete, Verum structures and versions the comparator evidence set — ready to feed the dossier phase without rebuilding from scratch per country.
Adjacent input
EPAR is a key input to Verum V2 corpus. Published regulatory assessments reveal accepted evidence standards per indication and become structured data.
V1 — Core value prop 🎯
EvidenceUnit DB → localisation engine → country dossier with full citation trail. The entire V1 architecture is built for this phase. HITL at every gate. Replaces manual Word/Excel → consultant cycle per country.
V2+ deferred
CIPM pricing data restricted — correctly deferred. Long-term: structured outcome data (OS HR, PFS, QoL) from EvidenceUnits could feed economic models directly, connecting evidence layer to pricing layer.
Adjacent
RWE publications eventually become EvidenceUnits — feeding updated dossiers for label extensions or re-assessments when MEA obligations come due.
V2 — High value 🎯
Cross-indication reuse: an EvidenceUnit from lung cancer may partially satisfy the PICO for bladder cancer in the same drug program. Verum surfaces reuse opportunities — compressing timelines and SLR costs on new submissions in the same therapeutic area.
